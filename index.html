Our Family

From a kitchen in Michoacán
to a table in Rock Island.

Taqueria Roly's was founded by Roly Gerardo and his wife Martha in 2015, bringing the homemade, traditional food of their hometown in Michoacán, Mexico to the Quad Cities. After about seven years serving Moline, the family taqueria relocated to Rock Island in 2022.

Martha hand-presses the corn tortillas and develops the dishes alongside Roly — including the slow-cooked birria that anchors their signature quesabirria. Everything is made to order, with a salsa bar of varying spice levels and generous portions at honest, neighborly prices.
